Decided

  1. Option 3 is the default for anyone who already has data — real numbers shown instantly, dimmed, with a single background-refresh pill.
  2. Option 4's ring-only treatment, applied per-card as Option 5 is the cold-start state. One indicator only (spinning ring — the progress bar was dropped as redundant with it).
  3. Why per-card, not one full-page block: AnalyticsView already fetches its 7 sections independently and today shows each as soon as it resolves — Allocation/TER/Benchmark typically land in well under a second, Category Ranking/Score is the slow leg (network NAV warm + peer-universe CAGR calc). On cold start, per-household precompute writes each section's row as it finishes, not all-at-once — so a single full-page "Setting up your analytics" block would sit idle for the fast sections' data for no reason. Option 5 keeps that same instant-when-ready behavior: each card shows real data the moment its own precompute lands, and only the still-computing cards show the ring, scoped to that card, not the page.
  4. Skip 1 and 2 as separate states — both were "pretend it's fast" treatments for a wait that, on cold start, genuinely isn't fast; 5 tells the truth per-section instead, and 3 covers the fast path for everyone else.
